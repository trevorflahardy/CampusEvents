About the Workshop

Unleash your inner creator in our exclusive Pottery & Sculpting Workshop. Whether you're a seasoned ceramicist or picking up clay for the first time, this session is designed to ground your creativity through tactile exploration.

We will cover fundamental wheel-throwing techniques, hand-building strategies, and the delicate art of glazing. All materials including premium stoneware clay, aprons, and professional sculpting tools will be provided. Your finished pieces will be fired in our kilns and ready for collection two weeks after the event.
